01 / HOW WE WORK

Start with a real problem.

Some ideas begin as a question. Others begin with a frustrating handoff, an information gap, or a tool that could work better.

01 / EXPLORE

Understand the challenge

Get closer to the people, decisions, and everyday realities connected to a health problem.

02 / RESEARCH

Ask better questions

Investigate what is known, what is missing, and what would be useful to learn next.

03 / BUILD

Try practical approaches

Develop and test tools or ways of working that could help people and care systems.

ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLES / NOT ACTIVE PROJECTS

What the work could look like.

Sample concepts to show the kinds of questions the Labs might explore. These are placeholders, not current research or commitments.

SAMPLE CONCEPT 01

Closing the loop after a test result

Problem to explore A result is available, but the next responsible person or action may not be clear.

Possible first step Map the handoffs from result to decision and follow-up.

SAMPLE CONCEPT 02

Health information people can use

Problem to explore People receive information that is difficult to interpret or act on.

Possible first step Explore clearer explanations and ways to support better questions.

SAMPLE CONCEPT 03

Making prevention easier to navigate

Problem to explore Preventive care can be hard to understand, access, or fit into everyday life.

Possible first step Identify friction points and test a practical service or information improvement.
